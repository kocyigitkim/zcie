## Problem found after the deployment: queries during the vector fill
After the restart of the deployment, the 30-stage memory check gave 9/17, then 14/17, and 17/17 (159 tokens per question) after the vector fill ended. During the fill, a query took about 0.7 s, and the hook (2 s limit) printed nothing for some prompts. Cause: the fill took the write lock of the vector index once for each of the 302,000 vectors, and each query held the read lock for a scan of a whole project; both waited for each other (fill 404 s under query load, 68 s with no load).

Fix (part of D-077): the fill computes the vectors with no lock and stores them in batches of 512 under one write lock (`VectorIndex::insert_many_if_absent`). Measured on the store of the user, with a query every 0.5 s during the fill: fill 80 s; 146 queries during the fill, p50 27 ms, p95 103 ms, max 556 ms.
